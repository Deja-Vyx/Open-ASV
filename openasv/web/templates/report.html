Scoring rules

  • A component fails when it has a vulnerability with a CVSS base score of 4.0 or higher, or a vulnerability in an automatic-failure category, regardless of score.
  • Purely denial-of-service vulnerabilities are not ranked as failures.
  • NVD or engine-supplied CVSS scores are used where available; otherwise the policy assigns a CVSS v3.1 vector per category (source shown on each finding).
  • Special notes and discovered components must be resolved by the scan customer before the scan can pass. Inconclusive scans are reported as failed until resolved.

Limitations

  • The scan is unauthenticated, as for ASV scans; content behind authentication was not tested.
  • Version-based findings may be false positives where vendors back-port fixes; dispute them with patch-level evidence.
  • Business-logic, access-control and procedural controls are outside the scope of an external vulnerability scan.
